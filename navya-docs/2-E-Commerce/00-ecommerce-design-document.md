## Platform rules

- Sign-up: members confirm they are 18 or older and accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Notice. The time and the version are stored.
- Listing: title up to 80 characters, price Rs 0 to Rs 1,00,00,000, up to 4 photos (JPEG, 600 KB each after the browser resizes), description up to 2,000 characters.
- A listing expires after 60 days. A seller can mark it sold or available again, edit or delete it.
- One chat per buyer and listing. Messages up to 2,000 characters. A seller cannot chat with themselves.
- Reports: one per member per listing. The Navya team removes the listing or dismisses the report.
- Boost: one fee, 7 days, stacks if bought again.
