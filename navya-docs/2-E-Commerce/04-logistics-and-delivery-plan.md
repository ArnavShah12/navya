# Logistics and delivery plan

> Subject: E-Commerce | Deliverable: logistics or delivery plan | Data: plan, nothing built beyond the safety note

## Now: in-person handover

The buyer and seller agree a place and time in the chat. The site shows a note on listing and chat pages: meet in a public place, check the item before paying, never pay in advance. Navya does not touch the goods or the money.

Why: it keeps cost and legal load low. Items are low value and students live near each other.

## Possible later steps

| Step | What it needs |
|---|---|
| Campus pick-up points | Agreement with a college or a shop near the gate |
| Courier for other cities | Partner with a courier aggregator, label printing, address collection (more personal data) |
| Returns | A written policy. In-person deals have none today: buyers check before paying |

## Risks

No-shows, meeting strangers, disputes about condition. Controls: reports, condition labels, photos (up to 4), seller profile with other listings.
