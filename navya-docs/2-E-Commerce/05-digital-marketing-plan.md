# Digital marketing plan

> Subject: E-Commerce | Deliverable: digital marketing plan | Data: plan and targets, not results

| Channel | Action | Cost | Target for the pilot (assumption) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campus ambassadors | One per hostel or club, post their own items first | Small perk, no salary | 10 ambassadors, 20 listings in week 1 |
| Instagram | Before-and-after posts of listings, student stories | Free | 500 followers |
| College WhatsApp groups | Share a city page link when a group asks | Free | 5 groups |
| Referral | A member who brings a seller gets a free boost | Cost of one boost | 20% of new sellers |
| Search | Page titles and descriptions for "second hand <item> <city>" | Free | Not measured |
| Moving-out season | Posters in hostels in May and December | Print cost | 2 colleges |

Budget for the three-month pilot: Rs 3,000 (posters Rs 1,000, ambassador perks Rs 1,500, referral boosts about Rs 490), an assumption set in `../1-Startup-Policies/04-funding-plan.md`.

Messages: "Sell what you do not wear", "No phone number needed", "On your campus, near you".

Metrics: sign-ups per week, listings per ambassador, chats per listing, boost purchases. All come from the app log and the database, see `07-operations-and-metrics.md`.

Rules we follow: no fake reviews or fake listings to look busy, no countdown timers or pressure messages (see the dark-pattern list in `06-legal-and-security-checklist.md`), no emails to people who did not sign up.
