## 1. What Navya is

Navya is a classifieds site for pre-loved clothes, shoes, bags and accessories. Members post listings, search, save items and chat with each other. Navya does not buy, sell, hold or deliver anything. Deals are made between members, in person.

Navya is run by Arnav Shah and Dipanshu Parmar, students of JG University, as a B.Tech course project. It is not a registered company. You can reach us through the Grievance Officer (see the Grievance page).

Navya is in a pilot. The site can be offline or change without notice. Boost payments use a test payment gateway: **no real money moves and no card is charged.**

## 6. Boosts

A boost pins your listing to the top of the home page for 7 days. The price is shown before you pay: Rs 49, and Rs 39 on Saturdays and Sundays (Indian time). Buying a second boost adds 7 days after the first ends. A boost is a one-off purchase, not a subscription.

If a payment is declined, you are not charged and nothing is pinned. If a payment succeeds, the boost starts at once and is not refundable, except that if it was charged and not applied we will apply it or refund it. In this pilot the gateway is a test gateway and no money is taken. When real payments start, GST will be added where the law requires, and these terms will be updated first.
