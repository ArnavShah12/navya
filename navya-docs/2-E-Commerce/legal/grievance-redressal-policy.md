## What you can complain about

- A listing or a message that breaks the Terms of Use or the law (fake, counterfeit, abusive, illegal).
- Your account, or a decision we took on a report or on your listing.
- Your personal data: a request to see, correct or erase it, or a complaint about how it is used.
- A boost payment that was charged and not applied.
- Safety, or anything else about how Navya works.

## How to complain

For one listing, use the Report button on its page. For everything else, email the Grievance Officer with:

- the email address of your account (we only act on data requests that come from it);
- the link to the listing, if there is one;
- what happened and what you want us to do.

## What we promise

- We acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours and give you a reference number.
- We answer with a decision and our reasons within 15 days.
- We review reports about unlawful or harmful listings within 48 hours and remove a listing first if it is clearly unlawful.

These times are at least as strict as the ones in the law: the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, rule 3(2), set 24 hours to acknowledge and 15 days to resolve, and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 set 48 hours to acknowledge and one month to resolve.

## What we do

We can remove a listing, warn a member, suspend or close an account, or dismiss the complaint. Every moderation action is written to an audit log with the time and the person who took it. We keep complaint records for one year after they are closed.

## If you disagree

Ask for a second review. Dipanshu Parmar, who did not handle the first one, looks at it and answers within 15 days. You can also complain to the National Consumer Helpline or a consumer commission about a consumer matter, to the Data Protection Board of India about personal data, or go to court.

## Notice of unlawful or infringing content

If you own the rights to something on Navya, or a court or government order requires its removal, email the Grievance Officer with the link, what is wrong, and your proof or the order. We act promptly once we have checked it. The member who posted it can reply and we will consider their reply.

## Not allowed on Navya

Counterfeit or copied goods, stolen goods, anything illegal to sell in India, items outside clothes, shoes, bags and accessories, weapons, drugs, adult content, abuse and threats, spam and fake listings, and asking for payment in advance. The full list is in the Terms of Use.
