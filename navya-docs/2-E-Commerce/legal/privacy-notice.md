# Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-25. This explains what personal data Navya collects, why, who sees it, where it is kept, and what you can do about it. It follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 1. Who is responsible

Navya, run by Arnav Shah and Dipanshu Parmar, students of JG University (see the Terms of Use). For anything about your data, write to the Grievance Officer: **Arnav Shah, arnavshah0906@gmail.com**.

## 2. What we collect, and why

- **Account:** your email, name and city, and a password that we store only as a bcrypt hash. Purpose: to create and secure your account and to show your name and city on your listings.
- **What you post:** listing details (title, price, category, condition, size, city, description), up to 4 photos per listing, and the messages you send in chats. Purpose: to run listings, search and chat.
- **Reports you file:** the listing and your reason. Purpose: moderation.
- **Boost orders:** the listing, the amount, the status and a request key. No card number is ever typed into or stored by Navya. Purpose: to apply boosts and keep order history.
- **Your consent:** the time you ticked the box at sign-up and the version of these documents you accepted.
- **Technical data:** your IP address, which the server uses briefly to limit repeated requests and which the web server may keep in its logs, and request log lines (method, page, result, time, your member id). We do not log your password, login token or message text.

We do not collect your phone number, location, contacts or payment card details. We do not run advertising, tracking or analytics, and we load no third-party scripts.

## 3. Who sees it

- Other members see your name, city, listings and photos. They never see your email. Only the two people in a chat see its messages.
- The Navya team can see listings and reports. The admin pages do not show messages, but a team member with access to the database can read them.
- Our hosting provider, Amazon Web Services, stores and processes the data for us (virtual server, database and photo storage). It acts on our instructions.
- We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

## 4. Where it is kept

On Amazon Web Services servers in Sydney, Australia. This means your data is transferred outside India. Photos are stored in a private bucket. The database is not open to the internet.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

Navya sets no cookies. After you log in, your browser keeps a login token in its local storage for up to 12 hours or until you log out.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Account, listings, photos, favourites and messages: until you delete them or your account. A listing leaves search after 60 days but stays in My listings until you delete it.
- Boost orders: kept after your account is deleted, without your name or email, as a record of payments.
- Moderation actions: the audit log keeps who did what and when.
- Backups: the database makes automatic backups. Data you delete can stay in a backup for a short time set by the provider (about a week by default, not confirmed for this account).
- Server logs: kept on the server until it is deleted. They are not rotated automatically.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold and how we use it;
- correct, complete or update it;
- erase it, which also ends your account (this is also how you withdraw your consent);
- address a grievance about how we handle it;
- name another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act.

Write to the Grievance Officer from the email address on your account. We confirm your identity, acknowledge your request within 24 hours and finish within 15 days.

When we delete your account we remove your listings, photos, favourites, chats, messages and reports, and we replace your name, email and city on the account with neutral text so it cannot be linked to you. Boost orders and audit-log lines stay without your personal details.

## 8. Children

Navya is for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we find that we have, we delete it.

## 9. Security

The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. The database and photo storage are private, and only the team can reach the server. No system is completely safe. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

## 10. Complaints

Write to the Grievance Officer first. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 11. Changes

When we change this notice we change the version date and ask you to accept the new version if the change matters to your rights.
