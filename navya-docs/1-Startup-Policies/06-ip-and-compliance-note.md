# IP and compliance note

> Subject: Startup Policies | Deliverable: IP and compliance note | Data: legal points are the team's reading of public rules, not legal advice. A lawyer should review before launch.

## Intellectual property

| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| Source code | Written by the team. Third-party libraries and their licences are in the README credits table. Javalin, Jackson, HikariCP, AWS SDK and JUnit are Apache 2.0, jBCrypt is ISC, java-jwt is MIT, PostgreSQL JDBC is BSD-2, Logback is EPL/LGPL, k6 is AGPL-3.0 and is run as a tool, not linked. |
| Starting prototype | The first version (HTML, CSS, JavaScript with Supabase) was written by a team member. It was ported to Java in this project. |
| Name and logo | "Navya" is a common word and name. No trademark search has been done. Do one before spending on branding. No logo exists beyond text. |
| Member content | Members keep rights to their photos and text. The Terms of Use (`../2-E-Commerce/legal/terms-of-use.md`) say they give Navya a free licence to show them on the site while they are posted. |
| Demo photos | The demo listings use plain coloured pictures generated by `Seeder`, not copied from anyone. |

## Compliance

| Law or rule | What it asks | State of Navya |
|---|---|---|
| IT Act 2000 and the Intermediary Guidelines 2021 | Intermediaries act on notice of unlawful content, publish rules, name a grievance officer | Report button and moderation exist. Terms of Use, grievance officer, takedown notice process and timelines are written and published (`../2-E-Commerce/legal/`). Not reviewed by a lawyer. |
| Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Rules 2025 (notified 13 to 14 November 2025; some parts start at once, others in phases, check the Gazette text) | Ask consent, collect only what is needed, let people delete data, protect it. Verifiable parental consent before processing the data of anyone under 18. | Data collected: email, name, city, password hash, listings, messages. No tracking or ads. Privacy Notice and consent box are in place, with the consent time and version stored. Erasure and export are done by the Grievance Officer on request, not self-service. |
| Age | Children's data needs a parent's consent | Sign-up has a required 18+ and terms box. There is no age check beyond the member's own confirmation. |
| Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 | Every e-commerce entity sets up a grievance mechanism and names a grievance officer, who acknowledges a complaint within 48 hours and resolves it within a month. The officer's contact details must be shown prominently. | Seller name and city are shown. The officer (Arnav Shah), the process and the page (`/grievance.html`) are written and published. Acknowledge within 24 hours, resolve within 15 days. |
| Prohibited and counterfeit goods | Platforms should not carry them | Categories are limited to clothes, shoes, bags, accessories. Counterfeits can still be posted: reports are the only control. |
| Payments (RBI rules) | Card data and payment aggregation are regulated | Navya never sees card data. Real payments would go through a licensed gateway with hosted checkout. |

Sources for these rules: `research-evidence/real-world-benchmarks.md`. Gaps are repeated in `../2-E-Commerce/06-legal-and-security-checklist.md`.
