# Research instruments and results

> Subject: Startup Policies | Deliverable: customer research instruments | Data: the instruments are real and ready to use. The results in this folder are **simulated** (see `README.md`).

## Interview guide (15 minutes, one person at a time)

1. What was the last thing you sold or bought second-hand? Where? Walk me through it.
2. What went wrong, or took too long?
3. How do you decide whether to trust a stranger? Where do you meet?
4. What do you do with clothes you no longer wear?
5. Would you pay to make an item sell faster? How much, and for what kind of item?
6. What would make you stop using a site like this?

Notes to take: exact words, what they did (not what they say they would do), and any workaround. Ask permission, tell them it is a student project, keep no names.

## Survey (2 minutes, 12 questions)

Age, city. Sold online before? Bought second-hand before? Main platform. Biggest problem with it. Interest in a campus-friendly resale app (1 to 5). Safety worry when meeting a stranger (1 to 5). Would you pay to pin a listing (yes, maybe, no)? Highest fee you would accept. Items you could list now.

Raw simulated rows: `survey-responses.csv`. Summary: `survey-summary.md`. Interviews: `interviews.md`. Usability: `usability-results.md`. Pilot: `pilot.md`.

## Running the research for real

To run the interviews and survey with students at JG University, put the notes and the exported rows in this folder and update `survey-summary.md`. The generator script is `navya/tools/simulate_survey_and_usability.py`.
