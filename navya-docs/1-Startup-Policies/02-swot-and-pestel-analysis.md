# SWOT and PESTEL analysis

> Subject: Startup Policies | Deliverable: SWOT and PESTEL | Data: team judgement, to be checked against real research

## SWOT

| | Helpful | Harmful |
|---|---|---|
| Inside | **Strengths:** working prototype with tests (84 automated, 40 browser checks). Low running cost at small scale. Chat without phone numbers. Fashion-only focus keeps the app simple. | **Weaknesses:** no users yet. No payment gateway, so revenue is not proven. Team of students with little time. Search is a plain text match. No seller identity checks. |
| Outside | **Opportunities:** large student population that already resells informally. Growing interest in second-hand fashion. Campus-by-campus launch. | **Threats:** OLX, Instagram and Facebook already hold the habit. Scams can destroy trust quickly. Prohibited or counterfeit items create legal risk. Cloud cost per user is high until traffic grows (see cost estimate). |

## PESTEL

| Factor | Points |
|---|---|
| Political | Government support for start-ups (Startup India, DPIIT recognition) helps, but only once the team is a registered company. |
| Economic | Students have little money, so low prices and a small fee matter. UPI is the normal way to pay in person. |
| Social | Second-hand buying is becoming more accepted among students. Safety when meeting strangers is the top worry (simulated survey mean 4.1 of 5). |
| Technological | Most users are on phones, so the site is mobile first. Cloud and managed databases make a small team viable. |
| Environmental | Reuse of clothes reduces waste. This is a marketing point, not a measured claim. |
| Legal | IT Act 2000 and the Intermediary Guidelines (platform duties, takedown, grievance officer). Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (consent, purpose limits). Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020. See `06-ip-and-compliance-note.md`. |
