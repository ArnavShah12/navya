# Lean Canvas and Business Model Canvas

> Subject: Startup Policies | Deliverable: Lean Canvas and Business Model Canvas | Data: assumptions, to be tested

## Lean Canvas

| Block | Navya |
|---|---|
| Problem | 1. Selling used fashion online gets few offers. 2. Chatting on WhatsApp shares your phone number. 3. Searching by city and category on general sites is clumsy. Existing alternatives: OLX, Instagram, WhatsApp groups, Facebook Marketplace. |
| Customer segments | Students and early-career members, 18 to 26, in Indian cities. Early adopters: students about to move out of hostels (they sell, then buy). |
| Unique value proposition | Resell your fashion near you, chat without giving out your number. |
| Solution | Fashion-only listings, city search, in-site chat, report button with human review, optional boost. |
| Channels | Campus ambassadors, Instagram, college WhatsApp groups, hostel notice boards, referral by existing sellers. |
| Revenue streams | Boost fee (Rs 49 for 7 days). Later: verified-seller badge, local brand ads. |
| Cost structure | Cloud hosting (see cost estimate), moderation time, marketing, payment gateway fee when live. |
| Key metrics | Listings posted per week, share of listings marked sold, chats started per listing, boost purchases per 100 listings, weekly active members. |
| Unfair advantage | None yet. Possible: campus-by-campus trust, which a general site cannot copy quickly. |
